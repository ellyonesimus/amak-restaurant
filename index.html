Explore Our Menu

From comforting rice dishes to traditional soups and tasty grills, explore some West African favourites you can ask us about.

🍚

Rice Dishes

Colourful, satisfying favourites

  • Jollof Rice

    Rice cooked in a rich tomato and pepper base with classic West African flavours.

  • Fried Rice

    A colourful rice dish with mixed vegetables and savoury seasoning.

  • White Rice and Stew

    A comforting combination of fluffy rice and a rich tomato stew.

  • Rice with Grilled Chicken

    A hearty rice meal paired with flavourful grilled chicken.

🥣

Soups and Swallows

Traditional comfort food

  • Egusi Soup

    A rich melon-seed soup traditionally enjoyed with a swallow of your choice.

  • Okra Soup

    A beloved Nigerian soup with a distinctive texture and savoury flavour.

  • Vegetable Soup

    A hearty, greens-filled soup inspired by traditional West African cooking.

  • Fufu, Eba or Pounded Yam

    Classic swallow options to pair with a favourite soup, subject to availability.

🔥

Grills and Snacks

Smoky, savoury and satisfying

  • Grilled Fish

    Fish with a delicious grilled finish and a tempting blend of spices.

  • Suya

    A popular West African-style grilled meat snack with bold spice flavours.

  • Grilled Chicken

    A savoury favourite with a golden finish and rich seasoning.

  • Pepper Soup

    A warming, peppery broth inspired by a Nigerian classic.
